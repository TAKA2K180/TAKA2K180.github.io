RevLogic — Privacy Policy

Effective 25 July 2026 · RevLogic is developed by RCST Softwares (“we”, “us”).

RevLogic is an engine-building calculator app. This policy explains what data the app involves, who processes it, and the choices you have. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the app and show ads in the free tier.

Information stored on your device

  • The calculator inputs, saved specs, and engine build sheets you create are stored locally on your device (a private database inside the app).
  • We do not upload, sync, or have access to this data. Deleting the app removes it.
  • RevLogic has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email, or contacts.

Advertising (free tier)

The free tier shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google’s SDK may collect a device advertising identifier, IP address, and app-usage/diagnostic data. With your permission (see App Tracking Transparency below) ads may be personalized; otherwise they are non-personalized. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses data. Purchasing RevLogic Pro removes ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases (RevLogic Pro) are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; we only receive whether the purchase is active, so we can unlock Pro features.

App Tracking Transparency

On first launch the app may ask, via Apple’s standard prompt, whether it can access your device advertising identifier for personalized advertising. You can decline, and you can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Declining does not disable ads; it makes them non-personalized.

Children

RevLogic is intended for a general audience of vehicle and engine enthusiasts and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Turn off tracking for RevLogic in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Go Pro to remove ads entirely.
  • Delete the app to erase all locally stored data.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves; the effective date above will change accordingly.
